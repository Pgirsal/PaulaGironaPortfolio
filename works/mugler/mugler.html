OVERVIEW

A personal 3D advertising project inspired by Mugler's iconic Angel perfume. From modelling and texturing to lighting and final rendering, I developed the entire project in Blender, exploring how CGI can be used to create striking beauty advertising.

THE IDEA

Mugler has always had a very distinctive visual universe: futuristic, bold and almost otherworldly. I wanted to capture that identity by creating an atmosphere that feels both luxurious and surreal, where the perfume becomes more than just a product.

BRINGING THE CONCEPT TO LIFE

I created the 3D composition from start to finish, experimenting with materials, reflections, lighting and camera angles to achieve the desired aesthetic.

I focused particularly on the interaction between light and surfaces, using textures and carefully controlled highlights to give the visuals depth and a futuristic quality. Every element was designed to contribute to the same atmosphere.

FINAL RESULT

The result is a CGI advertising concept that combines beauty, technology and surrealism. This project gave me the freedom to experiment with every stage of production, from the initial idea to the final image, and explore how art direction can transform a product into an entire visual experience.
