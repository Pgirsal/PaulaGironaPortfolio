Brand identity and custom illustrated packaging system for Marvão tea.
Role: Visual Designer & Illustrator.

Overview The idea Bringing the concept to life Final result

OVERVIEW

Marvão is a herbal tea packaging project inspired by family traditions, memories and the feeling of home. Our challenge was to create three packaging sets based on the story of a grandmother who passed down her knowledge of herbal teas to her two daughters.

THE IDEA

We decided to give each woman her own packaging collection, reflecting her personality through design. I focused on the grandmother, exploring a nostalgic, warm and peaceful visual identity. I wanted the packaging to feel almost like a childhood memory, something familiar and comforting.

BRINGING THE CONCEPT TO LIFE

I believe every design decision should tell part of the story. The handwritten logo brings a sense of home, while the soft, dreamy illustrations evoke nostalgia and tranquillity. We experimented with unusual pyramidal structures, shiny paper and different textures to make the packaging feel as special as the story behind it. We also designed the opening experience so that the illustrations are gradually revealed, adding a little moment of surprise and discovery.

FINAL RESULT

The final collection brings together illustration, typography, materials and packaging design to create something that goes beyond simply holding tea. What I love most about this project is how every detail communicates something without needing words. From the illustrations to the shape of the box, everything works together to tell the grandmother's story and turn an everyday product into a more personal experience.
