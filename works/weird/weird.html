OVERVIEW

An experimental visual series exploring the uncanny, the surreal, and the expressive possibilities of generative digital imagery paired with editorial art direction.

THE IDEA

The project embraces strangeness and distortion—constructing dreamlike characters and environments that blur the boundary between fashion photography and digital hallucination.

BRINGING THE CONCEPT TO LIFE

Through iterative prompt direction, curation, and post-production refinement, each frame was shaped to evoke a distinct cinematic mood and tactile presence.

FINAL RESULT

The result is a cohesive five-part visual study into contemporary digital surrealism.
